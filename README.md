## 🎯 About the Module / អំពីម៉ូឌុល

**`universe_huokaingthara_about_about_content`** is the informational brand and content showcase module for **UNIVERSE HUOKAING THARA Group**. It introduces visitors, partners, and supporters to our multi-format content ecosystem, leadership and engineering governance, upcoming production house opening, and brand logo update due to copyright.

## ✨ Key Features / លក្ខណៈពិសេសចម្បង

* **🗂️ Interactive Section Filter Tabs:** Filter between *All Sections*, *Content Pillars*, *Leadership*, and *Milestones* to focus on specific areas of the group.
* **🎬 Content Ecosystem Overview:** Highlights core programming across *Movies & Short Films*, *Ghost Stories Khmer*, *Comedy & Pranks*, and *Music & Audio Visuals*.
* **🏛️ Leadership & Engineering Directory:** Presents executive leadership, governance, and development contributors clearly in both Khmer and English.
* **🌐 Dual Language Switcher (Khmer / English):** Instant toggle supporting Khmer-only, English-only, or bilingual display modes.
* **🌙 Dark / Light Cyberpunk Theme:** Styled with the signature **UNIVERSE HUOKAING THARA** holographic scanline aesthetic and adaptive theme switching.
